01 / Our history

A clear vision
since 2017.

Nolawi Consulting Architects & Engineers was established in 2017 to provide creative, functional design solutions in architecture and interior design. Registered as a professional architectural consultancy office, the firm delivers work that balances innovation, practicality and aesthetics.

The practice is grounded in professional expertise, human-centred design, attention to detail and sustainable thinking. Its portfolio spans residential, commercial and institutional projects, with a long-term ambition to be a trusted and inspiring consultancy.
